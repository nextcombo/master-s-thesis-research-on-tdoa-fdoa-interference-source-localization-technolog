# Master's Thesis: Research on TDOA/FDOA Interference Source Localization Technology [Attached MATLAB Code]

With the rapid development of wireless communication technology, satellite communication technology has been widely applied in various fields of society. Satellite communication serves as an important supplement and extension to ground communication, providing communication services in areas where ground base stations cannot be constructed, such as oceans and deserts. However, facing the increasingly complex electromagnetic environment and the intentional or unintentional interference with communication satellites, how to quickly and accurately locate the source of interference becomes particularly important.
Firstly, this paper provides a comprehensive review of passive positioning technology based on time difference and frequency difference in satellite communication systems, analyzing the research background and significance of passive positioning technology, while also examining the current development status of passive positioning systems. Subsequently, this paper mainly analyzes the estimation methods for TDOA (Time Difference of Arrival) and FDOA (Frequency Difference of Arrival) parameters in the joint interference source localization of TDOA/FDOA, proposes a method based on frequency domain phase interpolation for estimating FDOA parameters through linear interpolation of phase differences between spectral peaks, and simulation results show that this method can effectively improve the accuracy of parameter estimation. At the same time, a positioning solution method based on CAFI is proposed, and simulation results indicate that compared to the CAF method, it can significantly improve positioning accuracy.
This study investigates the problem of parameter estimation in the TDOA/FDOA joint positioning system, as well as the impact of parameter precision on positioning accuracy, proposing methods to improve parameter estimation accuracy, which has certain reference value for solving satellite interference source positioning problems.
Keywords: Passive interference source localization, TDOA, FDOA, Interference function
Introduction: This paper provides a detailed analysis of the interference source localization system based on the principles of dual-star positioning. It first analyzes the estimation methods for TDOA and FDOA parameters, then proposes an improved parameter estimation method to enhance precision, and finally conducts simulation analyses of the localization solution based on this method. The main research content of the paper is as follows:
1. In the parameter estimation method for interference source localization, independent and joint studies are conducted on TDOA and FDOA parameters, including their respective estimation methods and through simulation experiments for analysis and summary.
2. An improvement in the mutual fuzzy function parameter estimation method for joint localization is made, proposing a high-precision TDOA/FDOA joint estimation method. This method estimates the FDOA parameter by linear interpolation of phase differences around the peak of the mutual fuzzy value based on the mutual fuzzy function, effectively improving the accuracy of parameter estimation.
3. The method for solving the location of the interference source in joint localization is studied. By comparing the interference source's geographical location estimated using the parameter estimation method based on the mutual fuzzy function with the proposed high-precision parameter estimation method, the results of the localization are analyzed through experimental simulations.
The structure of the paper is as follows:
Chapter 1 analyzes the importance of satellite passive positioning technology and the current domestic and international research status of time-frequency difference joint localization, clarifying the main work of this paper.
Chapter 2 studies the parameter estimation methods in TDOA/FDOA joint localization, including separate estimation methods for TDOA and FDOA parameters and joint estimation methods. Several estimation methods for each parameter are introduced, and experimental simulation analyses are conducted for different parameter estimation methods.
Chapter 3, building on the mutual fuzzy function estimation method, analyzes its shortcomings, and presents a CAFI (Covariance Adjusted Interference Function) method for improving precision. Theoretical derivations and performance analyses are provided, followed by experimental simulations to compare its performance with the mutual fuzzy function.
Chapter 4 is the solution of TDOA/FDOA joint interference source positioning. Based on the previous TDOA/FDOA joint positioning technology, we have conducted positioning solutions for parameter estimation results based on mutual fuzzy function and CAFI method respectively. We selected an appropriate geostationary model to analyze the Taylor series positioning algorithm. Through comparing the positioning errors with different parameter estimation methods, we evaluated their performance.
The principle of mutual fuzzy function estimation for TDOA and FDOA is due to its accuracy and computational accumulation time related to the estimation of TDOA and FDOA parameters using mutual fuzzy function. To obtain sufficient precision in estimation results, it is necessary to accumulate a considerable number of sample points for mutual fuzzy function computation [50][51]. Therefore, directly estimating TDOA/FDOA based on the definition of second-order mutual fuzzy function has disadvantages such as high computational load and low algorithm efficiency. In fact, this method usually uses the fast Fourier transform method with higher computational efficiency to replace the related calculations in mutual fuzzy function. Thus, its positioning accuracy depends on the spectral resolution of discrete Fourier transform (DFT), which is affected by the length of DFT [52][53]. In this chapter, we propose a method called CAFI, which can improve the accuracy of FDOA while estimating TDOA. It uses linear interpolation of phase differences between adjacent CAF values around the peak frequency of CAF to estimate FDOA.
The CAFI method is implemented based on the traditional CAF method. As shown in Figure 3.1, for two signals with time difference and frequency difference, firstly, according to the noise correlation of the signal, choose the mutual fuzzy function based on FOS or the mutual fuzzy function based on SOS method. If the noise is correlated, we use CAF-FOS, otherwise, use CAF-SOS method. Then calculate the time delay D and put it into subsequent calculations.
